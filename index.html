2. High-Fidelity Wireframe

This detailed wireframe represents KBClub's content design, specifying combat-sports product choices, size selectors, weight specifications, pricing in Philippine Pesos (₱), and visual design guidelines.

Design Palette & Visual Guidelines

  • Primary Color: Crimson Red (#d32f2f) — header titles, primary buttons, and highlight accents.
  • Accent Color: Matte Black / Charcoal (#1a1a1a) — structural borders, strong headings, and combat aesthetic.
  • Neutral Colors: Crisp White (#ffffff) and Light Gray (#f9f9f9) — card backgrounds and product tables.
  • Typeface: Arial / Sans-Serif — bold uppercase headings for strong presence, regular for description copy.

Page Flow

  1. Home: Trainee or buyer arrives at KBClub, views the slogan banner, and identifies primary gear categories.
  2. Category Selection: Customer navigates to specific equipment sections (Gloves & Hand Wraps, Fight Apparel, or Protective Gear) and configures product sizes/weights.
  3. Cart: Customer reviews combat gear selections, adjusts equipment quantities, checks subtotals, and verifies the total price.
  4. Checkout: Customer enters delivery address in Baguio or other target cities, selects payment options (Card, COD, E-Wallet), agrees to fight gear terms, and submits the order.
